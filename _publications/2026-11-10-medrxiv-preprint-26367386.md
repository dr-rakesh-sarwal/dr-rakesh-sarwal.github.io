This preprint evaluates the real-world impact of a traffic-light framework for lifestyle modification in chronic disease management within an integrative health clinic setting. The model organizes health behaviours into red, amber, and green categories to support patient self-management, risk reduction, and sustained behaviour change.

Drawing on routine clinical observations, the manuscript examines how structured lifestyle guidance can be translated into practical, scalable care for patients with chronic disease. The paper highlights the potential of individualized, evidence-informed lifestyle intervention as a complementary strategy alongside conventional medical management, emphasizing feasibility, patient engagement, and clinical relevance in day-to-day practice.

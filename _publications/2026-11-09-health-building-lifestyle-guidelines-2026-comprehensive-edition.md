Health Building Lifestyle Guidelines (2026 Comprehensive Edition) is a practical, referenced, comprehensive guideline for lay readers and health professionals. It BUILDS ON, and does NOT rename, replace or supersede, the 2025 Health Building Lifestyle Guidelines (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15814357, 5 July 2025). The main PDF is a 9-page LANDSCAPE BROCHURE with enlarged body type throughout, one topic per page and clickable links. It reproduces the 2025 guideline IN FULL (English, Hindi and its own reference list) on pages 2-4, each captioned with the 2025 DOI; the 12 reference URLs on reproduced page 4 are live links as well. The Title uses a SINGLE ACLM blue header in two lines, and EVERY URL link in the brochure uses that same deep blue. The three PURE traffic-light colours appear as three disks on page 1 (the 21-point key) and on ALL content headers (Spices = YELLOW, Yoga = RED); the Six-pillars page carries the brighter original ACLM six-pillar chips, with published ACLM pillar language in roman type, the 2025 21-item guideline in italics, and an explicit attribution footnote. Two images from the Sutra Health YouTube channel (infusion, Yoga) sit on the right of their pages. Page 8 is one merged evidence table listing the CONDITIONS for which there is evidence and THE PRACTICES THAT WORKS, colour-coded PREVENTION / TREATMENT / REMISSION-REVERSAL (refs [30] DiRECT, [31] Ornish), followed by two selected meta-analyses on prevention ([32] Kyu, [33] Reynolds); the separate evidence pages and the redundant Safety page were consolidated into it. The References hyperlink the reproduced 2025 handout's own list [1]-[12] on p4, note the first disclosure (8 Sep 2025 [5]), and run to 33 numbered references. This record's own DOI (10.5281/zenodo.24090390) is printed on page 1 of the PDF. No ACLM logo is used and no endorsement is implied. CC BY 4.0 is selected from Zenodo's default licence options. Contents: (1) The updated 21-item traffic-light Health Building Lifestyle Guidelines is being made available for open, public use with the CLARIFICATION that the first seven (red, "avoid") items are reverse-scored / negatively counted; all cross-references use colour-disc group-relative numbering (Red 1-7, Green 1-7, Yellow 1-7); a link to the deposited 21-item-assessment.csv (21 bilingual items, one point each, with a TOTAL column showing the maximum score of 21); and a link to the free public online 21-point assessment at https://www.lifequality.org.in/assessment (a self-assessment, monitoring and provider-administered tool that anyone can fill). (2) The six pillars of lifestyle medicine are listed on Page - 7. The published language follows the American College of Lifestyle Medicine (ACLM) protocol [6]. (3) Guideline-backed, general benefit statements for diet, physical activity / Yoga, sleep, stress, connection and substance avoidance, each cited to national and international guidelines and systematic reviews, are linked to ACLM pillars. (4) The 20-item Yoga asana and pranayama protocol, an elaboration of Yellow 1, with two safety sub-points, with a link to https://www.lifequality.org.in/services/therapeutic-yoga/yoga-asana-protocol, an archive video link (https://archive.org/details/surya-yoga-sequence) and its references - remission / reversal evidence is described as programme-level input (Yoga in combination), not Yoga stand-alone. (5) The home-prepared four-spice infusion (10 g each of coriander, fenugreek, cumin and cinnamon seed/bark = 40 g total, soaked overnight in 1 litre of water, do not boil; one 200 ml glass lukewarm first thing in the morning before Yoga; water replenished for up to three days, then prepared fresh), with the references used in the IBS case report and the clinic cohort paper, and a reference to its FIRST public disclosure (Sutra Health, "Wonder of Spices - In one glass every morning", 8 Sep 2025, https://www.youtube.com/shorts/6YnuyzY5RYg) are at Page - 5. Please note: The four-spice infusion is one optional component of a wider lifestyle pattern and was never evaluated separately. No efficacy, effectiveness or disease-modifying claim is made for the infusion, for any individual spice, for the Yoga protocol, or for the guideline as a treatment. The exact four-spice combination has NOT been clinically tested for efficacy; the spice references concern individual spices, different combinations, animal models or small pilot studies only. It is not a drug, a standardised extract, an investigational product, or a standard of care. The 21-item lifestyle score is for self-reflection, counselling and monitoring; it is not a diagnosis, clinical risk score, validated outcome measure, or substitute for professional medical advice. Keep taking prescribed medicines. One 200 ml glass is the serving; do not boil. No chemical or microbiological characterisation of the 3-day replenishment cycle has been published. No patient identifiers or patient-level data are included.

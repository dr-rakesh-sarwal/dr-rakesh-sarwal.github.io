Indexing is a mandatory requirement for the sustenance and progress of any scientific discipline. The AYUSH Research Portal (https://ayushportal.nic.in/) was created to make AYUSH research findings accessible in a user-friendly and index-based manner, helping bridge the gap between traditional Indian systems of medicine and modern evidence-based research. By 2010, despite the availability of more than 45 databases for complementary and alternative medicine, none was dedicated specifically to the indexing and dissemination of AYUSH literature. The portal was therefore designed to support search, retrieval, and classification of literature from Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa, and Homeopathy, while accommodating the ontological nuances of these systems. Over the past decade, it has expanded to include more than 30,000 articles and has become a valuable resource for researchers, practitioners, and policymakers seeking to strengthen global recognition of traditional Indian medical knowledge.

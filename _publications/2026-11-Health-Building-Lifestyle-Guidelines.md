The updated 21-item traffic-light Health Building Lifestyle Guidelines are being made available for open, public use with the clarification that the first seven (red, "avoid") items are reverse-scored. The guidelines are intended to support practical, prevention-oriented lifestyle choices.

Being added are the 20-item Yoga asana and pranayama protocol, an elaboration of Yellow 1, with two safety sub-points, with a link to the [yoga protocol page](https://www.lifequality.org.in/services).

The second addition to the guidelines is a home-prepared four-spice infusion (10 g each of coriander, fenugreek, cumin and cinnamon seed/bark = 40 g total, soaked overnight in 1 litre of water; one litre is consumed over the following day).

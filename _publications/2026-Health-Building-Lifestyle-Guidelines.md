"The updated 21-item traffic-light Health Building Lifestyle Guidelines are being made available for open, public use with the clarification that the first seven (red, "avoid") items are reverse-scored / negatively counted; all cross-references use colour-disc group-relative numbering (Red 1-7, Green 1-7, Yellow 1-7); a link to the deposited 21-item-assessment.csv (21 bilingual items, one point each, with a TOTAL column showing the maximum score of 21); and a link to the free public online 21-point assessment at https://www.lifequality.org.in/assessment (a self-assessment, monitoring and provider-administered tool that anyone can fill). 
Being added are the he 20-item Yoga asana and pranayama protocol, an elaboration of Yellow 1, with two safety sub-points, with a link to https://www.lifequality.org.in/services/therapeutic-yoga/yoga-asana-protocol, an archive video link (https://archive.org/details/surya-yoga-sequence) and its references. The addition to the guidelines is a home-prepared four-spice infusion (10 g each of coriander, fenugreek, cumin and cinnamon seed/bark = 40 g total, soaked overnight in 1 litre of water; one 200 ml glass lukewarm is to be  taken first thing in the morning before Yoga. The method of preparation are described in a video at - "Wonder of Spices - In one glass every morning" dated 8 Sep 2025 at https://www.youtube.com/shorts/6YnuyzY5RYg."

## Overview

A traffic light approach to lifestyle change provides a practical framework for managing non-communicable diseases (NCDs) through evidence-based lifestyle modifications.

### The Traffic Light System

- **Green (Go)**: Healthy choices to encourage daily
  - Fruits, vegetables, water
  - Regular physical activity
  - Stress management practices
  
- **Yellow (Caution)**: Choices to consume/do in moderation
  - Occasional sweets or fried foods
  - Limited screen time
  - Moderate caffeine intake

- **Red (Stop)**: Behaviors to limit or eliminate
  - Sugary drinks and processed foods
  - Smoking and tobacco use
  - Sedentary lifestyle

### Evidence Base

This approach has been demonstrated to be effective in:
- Improving patient compliance with lifestyle interventions
- Reducing burden of chronic diseases in resource-limited settings
- Empowering patients through simplified, memorable guidance
- Supporting primary care providers in NCD management

### Application in Indian Healthcare

This guideline is particularly suited for Indian primary care settings where:
- Traditional knowledge systems align with lifestyle-based prevention
- Resource constraints require simple, scalable interventions
- NCD burden continues to rise among working-age adults

The traffic light framework integrates modern evidence with traditional Indian wellness practices, making it culturally appropriate and sustainable for long-term behavior change.

This study maps COVID-19 metrics across parliamentary constituencies and districts in India, providing a more granular view of how the pandemic unfolded across different regions. By analysing spatial variation in key indicators such as case counts, testing intensity, and mortality, the paper shows that the burden of COVID-19 was highly uneven across the country. Such subnational variation is important for understanding geographic disparities in disease exposure, healthcare access, and public health response capacity. The findings underscore the need for locally tailored interventions, stronger surveillance, and more equitable allocation of health resources during public health emergencies.

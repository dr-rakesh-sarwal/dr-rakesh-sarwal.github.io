I shared the experience of starting and managing patients of chronic diseases at the Integrated Health Clinic. We have had over 2400 visits of over 1200 patients, 95% of which report having benefited from following the lifestyle regime taught at out clinic. Key elements of the lifestyle Regine include
- Lifestyle choices in terms of addiction free life, following the Sun cycle, exposure to the Sun. adequate hydration, stress free life, social networks.
- Dietary regulation, daily intake of a culinary extract of four spices
- Practice of Yoga every day
